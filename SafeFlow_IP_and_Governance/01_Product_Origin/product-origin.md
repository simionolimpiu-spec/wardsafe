# Product Origin

SafeFlow began as a simulation-only ward-flow safety prototype for NHS-facing clinical workflow validation.

The public-facing website is a pitch surface, not evidence of deployment. It should stay framed around:

- early-stage prototype status
- fictional or simulated data only
- governance readiness, not approval
- a future governed pilot, not live clinical use

The product story should keep one line consistent:

SafeFlow helps ward teams see operational risk earlier, coordinate escalation more clearly, and create a safer flow of clinical work from admission to discharge.
