# SafeFlow 3 Minute Pitch Script

## 0:00-0:20 - Who we are

We are building SafeFlow, a simulation-only ward-flow safety prototype. Oli is the primary creator, and Mia contributes clinical nursing workflow insight and safety framing.

## 0:20-0:50 - The frontline problem

Ward teams lose the thread between escalation, handover, discharge and staffing pressure. Risk can build quietly when visible work is split across systems and shifts.

## 0:50-1:30 - What SafeFlow does

SafeFlow makes that pressure visible earlier. It tracks ownership, escalation status, blockers and handover context in a human-in-the-loop workflow that is designed for fiction-first testing.

## 1:30-2:00 - Why this matters

It is not trying to replace clinical judgement. It is trying to help staff see risk earlier, coordinate better and reduce avoidable friction before it accumulates.

## 2:00-2:30 - Why CEP support is needed

The next step needs mentorship on clinical safety, IP, commercial validation, governance and evidence generation. CEP is the right place to shape the product into a governed NHS pilot path.

## 2:30-3:00 - What we will achieve in 12 months

In the next year we want a clear claims register, hazard log, safety case outline, shadow-mode evaluation and a single-ward pilot plan ready for approved review.

Closing message:

SafeFlow is a workflow safety product, not a finished clinical system. The goal is earlier visibility of ward pressure so teams can act before avoidable risk accumulates.
