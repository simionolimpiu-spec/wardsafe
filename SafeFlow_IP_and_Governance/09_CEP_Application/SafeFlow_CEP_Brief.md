# SafeFlow NHS CEP Brief

SafeFlow is an early-stage, simulation-only ward-flow safety prototype prepared for the NHS Clinical Entrepreneur Programme.

## Product

SafeFlow

## Primary creator

Olimpiu "Oli" Simion

## Clinical contributor

Mihaela "Mia" Simion, MSc RN

## One-line description

SafeFlow helps ward teams see operational risk earlier, coordinate escalation more clearly, and create a safer flow of clinical work from admission to discharge.

## Problem

Ward teams lose the thread between escalation, handover, discharge and staffing pressure. That creates avoidable friction, unclear ownership and delayed action.

## Solution

SafeFlow is a simulation-first workflow board with SBAR support, ownership tracking and a human-in-the-loop safety loop for detect, prioritise, escalate, coordinate, handover and learn.

## NHS value

The intended benefit is earlier visibility of flow friction, clearer handover and a governed path from prototype to pilot. No outcome claims are made yet.

## CEP fit

SafeFlow is seeking mentorship on clinical safety, IP, commercial validation, networking and evidence generation so it can move toward a governed NHS pilot.

## Governance roadmap

- DTAC readiness, not DTAC pass claims
- DCB0129 manufacturer safety work
- DCB0160 deployment and use planning
- DSPT, GDPR and data minimisation
- Accessibility and human oversight

## Pilot roadmap

- Prototype review with 3 to 5 ward reviewers
- Simulation and tabletop testing
- Shadow mode before any live clinical use
- Governance review with clinical and IG leads
- Single-ward pilot only if approved

## 12-month goals

- finalise the claims register
- complete the hazard log and safety case outline
- prepare a governance-ready evidence pack
- shape a shadow-mode evaluation route
- reach a single-ward pilot only after approval
