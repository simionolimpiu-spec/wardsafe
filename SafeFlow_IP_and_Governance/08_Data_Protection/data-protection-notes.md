# Data Protection Notes

## Current position

SafeFlow's public preview is simulation-first and should remain free of live patient data.

## Required boundaries

- Do not enter real patient identifiers, dates of birth, NHS numbers or notes into the public preview.
- Do not connect the public preview to live clinical systems.
- Do not export or share real patient data through the public pack.
- Treat all visible scenario data as fictional or simulated.

## Planned controls

- Data minimisation by design.
- Clear role-based access in any future private pilot.
- Audit trail for any significant action.
- DPIA, clinical safety and information governance review before any real-data pilot.
- Retention and deletion rules agreed before a live deployment discussion.

## Open questions

- What exact data would be needed for a supervised non-live pilot?
- Which roles would have access in a future private review?
- What retention period would be appropriate for pilot feedback and logs?
