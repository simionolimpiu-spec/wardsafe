# Hazard Log

| Hazard | Cause | Potential impact | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Users misinterpret simulation outputs as clinical advice | The interface or copy feels too real | Unsafe reliance on a public preview | Prominent simulation boundary, no live data, clear no-advice wording | Product owner | Open |
| Risk signal shown without adequate governance | The concept is shared before the review path is ready | Misuse or reputational damage | Keep the governance route visible and private packs aligned to claims control | Governance lead | Open |
| Overreliance on prompts | Staff trust the prompts instead of checking the patient context | Missed clinical judgement | Human-in-the-loop wording, reviewer prompts and explicit limitations | Clinical contributor | Open |
| Poor integration with ward workflow | The concept does not fit the way ward teams actually work | Low adoption and duplicated effort | Prototype review with ward staff and shadow-mode observation | Product owner | Open |
| Data protection risk if real patient data is entered | Users type real data into a simulation preview | Privacy or security incident | No-live-data warnings, data minimisation and input review before any pilot | Product owner | Open |
| Ambiguous escalation responsibility | The workflow makes ownership feel unclear | Delays or unsafe handoff | Clear ownership labels, acknowledgement cues and manual override language | Clinical contributor | Open |
| Incorrect public claim implying validation or NHS approval | Copy is too strong on the website or in a brief | False expectations with reviewers | Claims register review before publishing and page-level copy checks | Founder team | Open |
