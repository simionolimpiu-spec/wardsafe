# Competitor Landscape

This is a benchmark note, not a claim that SafeFlow competes directly with these products.

## Reviewed pages

| Product | Region | Page pattern | What to learn | SafeFlow difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Qventus | US | Strong enterprise hero, concise proof points, trust-heavy layout | Keep the headline short and the value proposition sharp | SafeFlow should stay nurse-led and simulation-first rather than automation-heavy |
| LeanTaaS | US | Capacity and flow language with enterprise proof | Show the workflow benefit without overexplaining the product | SafeFlow should avoid bed-management jargon and stay close to ward practice |
| DrDoctor | UK | Clear NHS-facing language and simple benefit framing | Use plain healthcare language and a direct CTA | SafeFlow should stay focused on escalation and handover, not generic patient comms |
| Alcidion | UK / AU | Workflow and clinical operations emphasis | Use modular surfaces and a more operational visual story | SafeFlow should keep the public preview lighter and narrower |

## Public-page pattern observations

- The strongest pages are short, structured and trust-aware.
- The best layouts use one main promise, a few proof points and one obvious CTA.
- Dense paragraphs and broad feature lists weaken the first impression.
- SafeFlow's public site is strongest when it reads as a workflow preview, not a product brochure.
