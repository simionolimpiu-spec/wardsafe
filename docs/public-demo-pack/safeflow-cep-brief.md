# SafeFlow CEP Brief

## Product
SafeFlow

## Founder / origin
SafeFlow is a nurse-led ward safety workflow concept. Olimpiu "Oli" Simion is the primary creator, and Mihaela "Mia" Simion provides the clinical nursing workflow contribution.

## One-line description
SafeFlow is a simulation-first workflow for ward teams to surface readiness, risk and escalation prompts before issues are missed.

## Problem
Ward teams manage risk across observation, handover, discharge and escalation. Those moments are often split across people, screens and shifts, which can leave context unclear and ownership uncertain.

## Proposed solution
SafeFlow gives teams a simple simulation-first view of readiness checks, risk signals, escalation prompts and audit learning. The aim is to make the workflow easier to test, discuss and improve before any real-world pilot is considered.

## Why now
NHS services are under pressure to improve flow, reduce invisible work and explain the governance path for digital tools. A bounded workflow concept can be reviewed earlier, before teams invest time in live integration or deployment planning.

## Why nurse-led
The concept comes from ward practice rather than a generic dashboard idea. The clinical contribution keeps the language grounded in nursing checks, handover clarity, escalation discipline and practical workflow fit.

## Simulation-first safety boundary
SafeFlow is a public simulation preview only. It uses fictional data, does not claim NHS approval, does not claim clinical validation, does not claim live deployment, and does not ask staff to rely on it for clinical decision-making. Any future live use would require the relevant clinical, information governance and safety reviews first.

## Pilot pathway
- Phase 0: simulation and workflow validation.
- Phase 1: prototype review with clinical staff.
- Phase 2: supervised non-live pilot, if governance and users support it.
- Phase 3: governance-led live implementation only if formally approved.

## Governance preparation
The current work is being prepared around public claims control, hazard logging, data protection, IP ownership, accessibility and a clear pilot evidence route. It is intended to be review-ready, not approval-claimed.

## What support is being sought
- CEP mentoring on clinical entrepreneurship and product shaping
- feedback on the clinical story and public claims
- advice on governance, pilot design and evidence expectations
- introductions to NHS reviewers, mentors or pilot advisors
