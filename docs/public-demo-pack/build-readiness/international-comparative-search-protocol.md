# SafeFlow — International Comparative Literature Search Protocol

Status: research-support document for the SafeFlow simulation-only prototype. Not a systematic
review; a structured, reproducible search protocol to gather comparative evidence and design ideas.

## Purpose

Compare how the UK and other Western systems, and East/Southeast Asian systems (Japan, the
Philippines, Malaysia, China, and where useful South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia),
recognise and respond to clinical deterioration, educate nurses (especially through simulation),
staff and skill-mix their wards, and build patient-safety culture — in order to identify (a) where
practice overlaps, (b) what each system does demonstrably better, and (c) concrete, transferable
ideas SafeFlow can build or teach next.

Framing note: SafeFlow is a simulation-only, nurse-led, review-support teaching tool. Everything
gathered here informs what is worth *simulating and teaching*; nothing here is a claim that SafeFlow
is clinically validated, nor that any one country's practice should be transplanted wholesale.

## Review question (PICo / SPIDER hybrid)

- **Population**: registered/ward/ICU nurses and the patients they monitor in acute hospital settings.
- **Phenomenon of interest**: recognition of and response to clinical deterioration; use of early
  warning scores (NEWS/NEWS2/MEWS/local EWS); rapid response / medical emergency / critical care
  outreach systems; simulation-based nursing education; nurse staffing and skill-mix; patient-safety
  culture and escalation behaviour.
- **Comparator/Context**: UK and Western systems (UK, Ireland, USA, Canada, Australia, NZ, Western
  Europe) versus Japan, Philippines, Malaysia, China (± South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore, Thailand,
  Indonesia); or single-country studies from those Asian systems that expose a transferable practice.
- **Outcomes / themes of interest**: detection accuracy and timeliness; failure-to-rescue and
  unplanned ICU admission; escalation behaviour and hierarchy/"speaking-up" barriers; educational
  effectiveness (knowledge, confidence, skill transfer); staffing ratios and outcomes; safety-culture
  scores; and any innovative model worth adapting.

## Inclusion / exclusion

Include: peer-reviewed primary research (quantitative, qualitative) and mixed-methods studies;
cross-national comparisons; validation/implementation studies of EWS/RRS in the target countries;
simulation-education trials and evaluations; nurse-staffing outcome studies; safety-culture surveys.
Systematic reviews included only as signposts to primary studies. Prefer 2015–2026; allow older
landmark work.

Exclude: opinion/editorial without data (unless a landmark commentary), purely animal/lab work,
non-acute settings unless directly transferable, and anything requiring reproduction of paywalled
full text beyond fair citation.

## Databases and routes

- PubMed (primary index; MeSH + free-text).
- RCN Library via WorldCat Discovery (`rcn.on.worldcat.org`) for nursing full text (member access).
- Open-access journals and PubMed Central for full text where paywalled elsewhere.
- UEA LibrarySearch noted but currently EBSCO-access-blocked for this account (OpenAthens OA-AP-4032-01).

## Search-string building blocks (combine with AND across lines, OR within)

- Deterioration: "clinical deterioration" OR "deteriorating patient" OR "failure to rescue" OR
  "afferent limb failure".
- Score/system: "early warning score" OR NEWS OR NEWS2 OR MEWS OR "rapid response" OR "medical
  emergency team" OR "critical care outreach".
- Education: simulation OR "simulation-based" OR "in-situ simulation" OR "virtual reality" OR
  debriefing OR "clinical reasoning".
- Workforce: "nurse staffing" OR "skill mix" OR "missed care" OR "care left undone" OR
  "nurse-to-patient ratio".
- Culture: "safety culture" OR "speaking up" OR "escalation of care" OR "hierarchy" OR "power distance".
- Geography: UK OR "United Kingdom" OR NHS; and Japan OR Japanese; Philippines OR Filipino;
  Malaysia OR Malaysian; China OR Chinese; plus "cross-national" OR "cross-cultural" OR international.

## Reusable prompt (for re-running this search later, by a person or an agent)

> Acting as an ICU/nursing academic, find peer-reviewed **primary research and mixed-methods**
> studies (2015–2026 preferred) that **compare** how the UK/Western systems and **Japan, the
> Philippines, Malaysia, and China** recognise and respond to clinical deterioration — including
> early-warning-score use, rapid-response systems, simulation-based nurse education, nurse
> staffing/skill-mix, and patient-safety/escalation culture. For each, extract: country/design/
> sample, the specific practice or finding, and **what a simulation-only nurse-education tool
> (SafeFlow) could learn or adapt**. Prioritise studies that expose a *transferable* idea or a
> measurable gap between systems. Return citations with DOIs and note full-text availability.
