# Future Discovery Register

A fuller IG/DPIA-lite checklist for these items is maintained outside the repo: `SafeFlow_Future_Discovery_IG_Checklist.md`.

This register parks ideas that are useful to discuss later, but they are not part of the current simulation-only prototype.

## 1. Patient feedback stations

**Why it is attractive**
- Gives a ward a simple way to note patient comments, themes, and concerns at the point of care instead of relying on memory or separate paper trails.
- Could help teams spot recurring experience issues sooner.

**Why it is out of scope now**
- It would handle identifiable patient information and therefore bring IG and DPIA obligations into scope.
- It would need a clear consent and retention model, plus a decision on how the information is shared or reviewed.
- Any routing or sharing across wards or services would raise multi-agency communications questions.
- SafeFlow is simulation-only and is not a live patient feedback platform.

**Open questions before any discovery build**
- Who would be the data controller?
- What lawful basis would apply?
- Would a DPIA be required?
- What consent model would cover collection, review, sharing, and retention?
- What IG approval would be needed?
- Would the clinical-safety-case scope need to change?

**Status**
- FUTURE DISCOVERY — not scheduled, not built, no code.

## 2. Ward social hub / community feature

**Why it is attractive**
- Gives ward teams a lighter place to share questions, local updates, and peer support without relying on scattered chat threads.
- Could reduce repeated coordination overhead and help build a visible community around ward practice.

**Why it is out of scope now**
- It would introduce identifiable patient or staff information and therefore bring IG and DPIA obligations into scope.
- It would need moderation, retention, and access rules before any real-world use.
- Any linking beyond the ward would create multi-agency communications questions that SafeFlow does not cover today.
- SafeFlow is simulation-only and does not include a live community space.

**Open questions before any discovery build**
- Who would be the data controller?
- What lawful basis would apply to posts and replies?
- Would a DPIA be required?
- What consent model would apply to participants and any copied content?
- What IG approval and moderation rules would be needed?
- Would the clinical-safety-case scope need to change?

**Status**
- FUTURE DISCOVERY — not scheduled, not built, no code.

## 3. Cross-team discharge coordination

**Why it is attractive**
- Gives nursing, therapy, pharmacy, and admin teams one place to see what is still blocking a discharge.
- Could reduce delays caused by fragmented task ownership and missing handover detail.

**Why it is out of scope now**
- It would use identifiable patient information and therefore bring IG and DPIA obligations into scope.
- It would require multi-agency communications rules and clear role boundaries across teams.
- It would overlap directly with live operational systems that SafeFlow must not claim to replace.
- SafeFlow is simulation-only and not a live coordination layer.

**Open questions before any discovery build**
- Who would be the data controller?
- What lawful basis would apply?
- Would a DPIA be required?
- What consent model would be needed for shared discharge notes or task updates?
- What IG approval would be required?
- Would the clinical-safety-case scope need to change?

**Status**
- FUTURE DISCOVERY — not scheduled, not built, no code.
