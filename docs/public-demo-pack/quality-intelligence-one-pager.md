# SafeFlow Quality Intelligence

For ward managers, practice educators, matrons, and clinical governance leads (Band 6/7/8a).

## The problem
When you need to pull together ward audit evidence, safety-huddle notes, and revalidation and learning evidence, it is often a manual job. The information sits in different places, the summary has to be stitched together by hand, and that takes time.

## What SafeFlow Quality Intelligence does
SafeFlow Quality Intelligence brings together the review cues, deterioration-pattern flags, and verified learning evidence already generated inside SafeFlow into one structured, exportable report: the Ward Quality & Safety Review export (SF-217).

In plain English, it collects what the ward picture has flagged, what needs a second look, and what learning has already been confirmed.

## What it does not do
- It does not score staff.
- It does not start escalation pathways; human review remains central.
- It does not replace the EPR, RRT/Call-for-Concern, or any live clinical or quality system.
- It is simulation-only today.

## What evidence would convince you?
What evidence would convince you this would save time and make your review pack easier to prepare? That is what the first evaluation session (SF-223) is there to test.

## Status
Prototype. Simulated data. Not in clinical use.
