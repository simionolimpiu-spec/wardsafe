# SafeFlow Stakeholder Demo Pack

## 1. One-line concept

SafeFlow is a nurse-led, simulation-only prototype that turns fictional patient documentation into review cues, ward comparison signals, and exportable learning summaries.

## 2. Problem SafeFlow Is Exploring

SafeFlow explores how ward teams can make documentation quality, handover completeness, discharge-readiness blockers, and review cues easier to see, discuss, and learn from.

## 3. What The Prototype Demonstrates

- A fictional patient and ward context.
- Deterministic review cues that remain explainable.
- Ward-level comparison through Hospital Insights.
- A structured Simulation Review Report.
- Presentation Mode for cleaner stakeholder demos.
- Copy and print export for discussion.

## 4. Live Demo Flow

Enable Presentation Mode -> choose Demo Scenario -> review patient cues -> open Hospital Insights -> generate Review Report -> copy or print export.

## 5. What Each Feature Shows

- Demo Scenario selector: switches between fictional ward and patient contexts.
- Patient cues: surfaces structured review cues such as documentation gaps, handover issues, and discharge-readiness blockers.
- Hospital Insights: compares one ward against the hospital simulation average and fictional peer wards.
- Review Report: brings the scenario, cues, comparison snapshot, and learning points into one structured summary.
- Copy / print export: creates a shareable demo artifact for stakeholder discussion.

## 6. Safety And Simulation Boundary

- SafeFlow is simulation-only.
- No real patient data is used.
- No live NHS systems are connected.
- No patient-identifiable information is used.
- It is not for clinical use.
- It does not provide diagnosis, treatment advice, risk prediction, or automated escalation.
- All review cues and comparison signals require human review.

## 7. Why This Matters For Nursing Documentation And Ward Learning

- Makes documentation quality easier to see in one place.
- Supports ward-level discussion about handover completeness and discharge readiness.
- Helps clinical educators and digital safety leads explain quality signals without using real patient data.
- Gives nursing leadership a fast way to review how structured documentation could support learning and improvement.

## 8. Future NHS/AWS Roadmap

SafeFlow currently demonstrates a simulation pathway from patient-level review cues to ward-level comparison and a structured review summary. Future work could place this behind approved NHS/AWS service boundaries, subject to information governance, clinical safety case development, and integration approval.

Planned direction:

- Approved data service boundary
- NHS/local trust integration
- AWS/Aurora storage
- Step Functions workflow orchestration
- Governed dashboarding and reporting
- Human-led review

## 9. Governance Readiness

Before any live pilot, SafeFlow would need:

- Clinical safety case development.
- DCB0129 / DCB0160-style safety case work where applicable.
- DPIA and information-governance approval.
- Caldicott and data protection review.
- Role-based access control.
- Audit logging.
- Data minimisation.
- Approved local trust pathways.
- Human-led nursing oversight.

## Short Close

SafeFlow is a simulation-only prototype for structured review support. It is designed to help nursing and transformation teams discuss documentation quality, comparison signals, and learning points without implying clinical automation or live NHS use.
