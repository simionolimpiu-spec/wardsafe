# SafeFlow 3-Minute Pitch

## 1. Opening problem
On wards, risk often hides in the gaps between observation, handover, discharge and escalation. Staff know the work is there, but the context is spread across people and systems.

## 2. Why this matters clinically
When context is unclear, teams can miss escalation cues, duplicate documentation or lose ownership between shifts. That creates pressure for staff and uncertainty for the next clinical decision.

## 3. What SafeFlow does
SafeFlow is a nurse-led ward safety workflow concept. It gives a simulation-first view of readiness checks, risk signals, escalation prompts and audit learning, so the workflow can be reviewed in a controlled way.

## 4. Why simulation-first
We are not claiming live clinical use. Simulation lets us test the concept safely, keep the boundary clear and hear from clinicians before any pilot is considered.

## 5. What has been built
We now have a public preview site, a one-page brief, a prototype path into the workflow and a governance pack that sets out claims, hazards and pilot thinking.

## 6. What support is needed next
We are looking for CEP mentoring, clinical safety feedback, governance advice and help shaping a small, sensible pilot pathway. The question is not, "Can we scale immediately?" It is, "Is this worth taking through the right NHS review steps?"

## Closing line
If the concept is useful, we'd like help refining the language, the pilot route and the evidence needed to move carefully to the next step.
