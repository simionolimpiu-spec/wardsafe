# Point-of-care hardware profile

Planning profile only, simulation-only prototype. Fictional patient data only; no real patient data. Human review required. No hardware is needed for the browser prototype and no procurement decision is made here. See the [master specification](safeflow-connect-voice-point-of-care-master.md).

## Fixed bedside terminal

Target class: 15-22 inch medical touchscreen, VESA/bedhead mount and cleanable enclosure. Connectivity direction: Wi-Fi 6/6E and Ethernet where appropriate. Input options: NFC/badge reader, 2D barcode reader and directional push-to-talk microphone. Optional ambient microphone only under the master specification's single-room/policy/sensitive-context gates. Output: speaker plus private headset/earpiece; confidential content must not use open speakers. Optional camera and privacy filter require local review before any future use.

## Handheld

Healthcare/disinfectant-ready enclosure, barcode reader, NFC, Wi-Fi, optional cellular, camera and push-to-talk controls. Hot-swappable or replaceable battery where useful. Future usability assessment must include gloves, one-handed use, readable patient context, safe docking and manual alternatives to voice.

## Research classes, not endorsements

Advantech, DT Research, Ascom Myco and Zebra Healthcare are hardware classes to investigate, not procurement endorsements or verified compatibility claims. No model, price, certification or availability is asserted. Future procurement must verify specifications and suitability with local teams.

## Infection control and privacy

Future assessment must verify approved cleaning agents, contact times, seams, mount/cable cleaning and shared-headset handling against local infection-control policy. A cleanable label alone is insufficient. Badge/wristband association must remain visible and explicit. Screen angle, privacy filters, room acoustics and inadvertent speaker output need bedside evaluation. Camera/microphone activation must be visible and human initiated. Lost devices, unattended terminals and shared workstations require appropriate local controls. Phase 1 has no actual readers, audio, camera, hardware driver or production authentication.
