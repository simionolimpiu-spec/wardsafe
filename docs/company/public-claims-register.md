# SafeFlow Public Claims Register

## Purpose

This register sets the public-language boundary for SafeFlow before anything is shared with NHS, CEP, community, investor, or other external stakeholders.

It is an internal control document, not legal advice and not a substitute for solicitor, accountant, or clinical governance review.

## Current Product Status

- SafeFlow is the product/platform name.
- Aurora Nursing Services Ltd is the existing company.
- The public simulation preview is a simulation-safe stakeholder discovery preview.
- Signals and risk suggestions remain placeholder-backed.
- This preview is not clinically validated decision support and must not be used for clinical decision-making.
- No real patient data is processed in the public simulation preview.
- No live NHS integration is present in the current preview.
- The product remains early-stage.

## Core Safety Boundary

The following boundary applies to all public copy:

- Simulation-only preview
- Fictional workflows only
- No real patient data
- Placeholder-backed signals and risk suggestions
- No clinical validation claim
- No NHS approval or endorsement claim
- No pilot-ready claim
- No production-ready claim

## Claims That Are Allowed Now

- SafeFlow is an early simulation-safe preview for workflow demonstration and stakeholder discovery.
- The preview is simulation-only and uses fictional workflows.
- Signals and risk suggestions remain placeholder-backed.
- No real patient data is processed in the public simulation preview.
- The preview shows how audit trail behaviour could support review and learning discussions.
- The preview can be used to discuss workflow shape, governance questions, and future integration needs.

## Claims That Require Qualification

These claims can be said only if the simulation boundary is stated at the same time:

- SafeFlow helps show how handover and discharge workflow could be structured.
- The preview makes readiness, escalation, and audit learning visible.
- The preview can support stakeholder discussion about workflow fit.
- The demo can help identify integration questions for later work.
- The audit trail view can help frame review and learning discussions.
- The workflow may feel familiar to ward teams.

Qualification required:

- Say that the above applies to a simulation-safe preview, not a validated clinical product.
- Say that any future use would require governance, validation, and integration work first.

## Claims That Must Not Be Made

Do not say or imply:

- clinically validated
- NHS-ready
- patient safety impact
- reduces harm
- predicts
- diagnosis
- diagnostic
- decision support
- validated model
- live patient data
- live NHS data
- integrated with NHS
- pilot-ready
- production-ready
- automated recommendation
- clinical algorithm
- AI-powered risk prediction

Also avoid:

- clinical deployment ready
- proven impact
- safe for clinical use
- approved for NHS use
- evidence of patient outcome improvement
- real-world diagnostic accuracy

## Evidence Needed Before Stronger Claims

If stronger wording is desired later, evidence should exist first for:

- Clinical validation or clinical safety case
- Formal governance and information-governance review
- Real integration work with documented system boundaries
- Stronger ML or read-model validation, if ML claims are later desired
- A defined pilot plan and acceptance criteria, if pilot language is later desired
- A clear legal and ownership structure, if investment or commercial claims are later desired
- Prospectively collected evidence for workflow improvement or patient-safety impact claims

## Stakeholder-Specific Wording

### A. General Public / Website Wording

SafeFlow is an early-stage simulation-safe preview for workflow demonstration and stakeholder discovery. It uses fictional workflows only and does not process real patient data. Signals and risk suggestions remain placeholder-backed, and outputs are not clinically validated or for clinical decision-making. Further governance, validation, and integration work would be needed before any pilot or clinical use.

### B. NHS / Clinical Stakeholder Wording

SafeFlow is being shown as a simulation-only preview so reviewers can discuss handover, readiness, escalation, and audit workflow shape. It is not for clinical decision-making and does not imply NHS readiness or live integration. Signals and risk suggestions remain placeholder-backed, and any future clinical use would need governance, validation, and integration review.

### C. CEP / Community Wording

This is a simulation-safe preview for workflow demonstration and stakeholder discovery. It shows how a ward workflow could be structured, but it does not claim clinical validation, safety benefit, or live patient-data use. This preview is not clinically validated decision support and must not be used for clinical decision-making.

### D. Investor Wording

SafeFlow is an early-stage product concept with a simulation-safe public preview. The preview demonstrates the workflow and product direction, but it does not show a validated clinical product, NHS deployment, or pilot readiness. Signals and risk suggestions remain placeholder-backed, and any stronger commercial or clinical claim would need evidence first.

### E. Developer / Technical Wording

The current preview is simulation-only and deliberately uses placeholder-backed providers for signals and risk suggestions. No real patient data is processed, and the preview is not a production or clinical system. Future integration, validation, and governance work would be separate from the current preview branch.

## Website / Demo Wording Guardrails

- Use "simulation-safe preview" or "simulation-only preview" first.
- Keep the safety statement visible near any simulated output.
- Use "workflow demonstration" and "stakeholder discovery" for the purpose of the demo.
- Use "placeholder-backed" or "fictional workflows only" when describing simulated output.
- Avoid headlines or body copy that sound like clinical claims, performance claims, or deployment claims.
- Do not bury the safety boundary in small-print only.

## Investor / CEP Wording Guardrails

- Say early-stage first.
- Say simulation-only first.
- Say placeholder-backed if talking about signals or risk suggestions.
- Say governance, validation, and integration work still need to happen.
- Avoid investment-ready, pilot-ready, de-risked, or product-market proof language unless evidence exists.
- Avoid phrases that sound like clinical outcomes, clinical adoption, or NHS readiness.

## NHS / Clinical Wording Guardrails

- Say not for clinical decision-making.
- Say no real patient data is processed.
- Say no live NHS integration is present in the current preview.
- Say the preview is for workflow demonstration and stakeholder discovery only.
- Avoid any wording that suggests clinical validation, safety improvement, diagnostic support, or an approved clinical tool.
- If discussing future work, frame it as future governance, integration, and validation work only.

## Review and Approval Process

Before sharing anything externally:

1. Check the copy against this register.
2. Confirm the exact safety statement is unchanged.
3. Confirm placeholder-provider status is still accurate.
4. Confirm the copy does not imply real patient data or NHS integration.
5. If a stronger claim is desired, list the evidence required before using it.
6. Review with a solicitor, accountant, or clinical governance adviser as appropriate before investor, NHS, or pilot conversations.
